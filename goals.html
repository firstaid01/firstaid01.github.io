MY STUDY GOALS

24 September 2026

While I want to learn ICT, I also need to be able to look into myself and realize both my positive and negative qualities to be able to succeed, both academically and personally.

My own SWOT:

Strengths

  • Perfectionism: I always try to do my best in every situation.
  • Eagerness to learn: I am always open to learning new things.
  • Self-awareness: I recognize my mistakes and I love constructive feedback and guidance.

Weaknesses

  • Perfectionism: I can get frustrated when something does not look or work the way I want it to.
  • Procrastination: I sometimes delay doing assignments until the last moment.
  • Anxiety: Exams and deadlines can make me feel stressed.

Opportunities

  • Internship opportunities
  • Lecturers who are supportive and who can help me with learning difficulties and procrastination.
  • Meeting new people and learning languages.

Threats

  • Difficulty focusing
  • Distractions from my phone, social media, or video games
  • Language barrier (No dutch knowledge as of now)

My mission for this year

My goal for this year is fixing my time-management and planning. I must be able to study without problems and as efficiently as possible, no more distractions, no more procrastination. I will be working extra hard to make sure I become a good software developer, starting from very basic knowledge or even from scratch. I will do extra tasks to understand entirely new or difficult concepts. I will write tiny programs in my free time to train my algorithmical and logical thinking. I will also go easy on myself as a perfectionist, I can’t expect myself to become a developer overnight.
