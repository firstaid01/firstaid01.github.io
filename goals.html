MY MISSION THIS YEAR

My goal for this year is fixing my time-management and planning. I must be able to study without problems and as efficiently as possible, no more distractions, no more procrastination.

I will be working extra hard to make sure I become a good software developer, starting from very basic knowledge or even from scratch.

I will do extra tasks to understand entirely new or difficult concepts. I will write tiny programs in my free time to train my algorithmical and logical thinking. I will also go easy on myself as a perfectionist, I can't expect myself to become a developer overnight.
