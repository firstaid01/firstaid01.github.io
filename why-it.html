WHY IT?

8 October 2026

As a child I spent most of my free time playing with lego. I loved building the sets, I loved building completely new things. This great feeling I got from creating has come with me all the way from my childhood to adulthood. I also enjoy overcoming obstacles when facing a challenge.

This I could feel very well when I was working as a car detailer. It might seem very unrelated, but let me explain. I enjoyed cleaning and polishing the dirty cars, as it felt like I am creating something brand new. While working, I also learned to take the customer’s needs very seriously, since I really wanted to provide the best service possible. In a sense, I also enjoy taking something and turning it into something even better.

I want to build useful/handy software that many people around the world can use, I even want to build games or build my own at one point. These, I would polish the way the community gives their feedback, happily seeing their satisfaction. As of writing this, my knowledge is fairly limited, although I am not frightened. I am new to programming and coding so I try to visualize and explain every new challenge or problem I come across until I fully understand what is happening. I love learning new things, so I am enjoying this journey so far.
