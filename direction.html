WHERE I SEE MYSELF IN THE FUTURE

8 October 2026

After finishing my course at HZ and becoming proficient in programming and problem solving, I would like to be working in the software engineering field.

I have heard that being a game developer is challenging, but I like the idea of creating and polishing a game based on feedback from the community who play said game. Although, I would surely need to work as a part of a game studio first before jumping headfirst into being a solo-developer.

Though first, I need to move towards that future. I need to finish my studies, become more confident with programming and build a portfolio that shows what I can do. I will need patience and consistent practice, especially when I face concepts that I struggle with in the beginning.
